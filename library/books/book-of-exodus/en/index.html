Book of Exodus

by The Bible

Summary

The second book of the Bible, recounting the liberation of the Israelites from Egypt under Moses and the covenant at Mount Sinai. It contains the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20), the foundational ethical and legal code of Western civilization, which has directly shaped natural law theory, Judeo-Christian ethics, and the philosophical grounding of property rights.
