Principles of Economics

by Menger, Carl

1871

Summary

Menger’s groundbreaking work that founded the Austrian School of Economics, introducing the theory of marginal utility and subjective value. This revolutionary text challenged classical economic theories and established the foundation for modern microeconomics.
