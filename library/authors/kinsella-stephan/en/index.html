Kinsella, Stephan

1965

American patent attorney turned anarcho-capitalist legal theorist and the most rigorous critic of intellectual property within the libertarian tradition. His 2001 essay Against Intellectual Property, published by the Mises Institute, argued that patents and copyrights violate property rights by granting state monopolies over non-scarce ideas, a position still fiercely debated in libertarian circles despite being made by a practicing patent attorney.
